## Design decisions

**The kinds of land** (`src/map/lands.ts`: `LAND_KINDS` gains `islands`, `delta`, `mountains`, `dryplain`, `woods`). Each is generated by `generateCoast(kind, size, seed)` from shared pieces (a new `src/map/kinds.ts`: the shore with its bays, capes and lobes, rivers and their channels, hills and ranges, woods, groves, outcrops, herds, districts) with its own recipe:
- **the Bay**: the hand-made coast, unchanged;
- **a sheltered bay** (edition 2): slice 42's layout made more varied: the bay anywhere along the shore and of more shapes (broad, narrow and deep, or two coves), the river rising anywhere along the north and reaching the bay from either side, the hills, woods and groves placed afresh; on larger lands a middle with features (a lake, uplands, a tributary river, an open plain);
- **islands offshore**: a wider sea with islands of every size off the coast and beyond the map's edge, a gentle shore with a cove for the quay;
- **a river delta**: low, green land; the river splits near the sea into channels that fan out to a coast pushed out into the sea, with reeds, marsh and pools between them; bridges join the parts; the hills, copper and marble at the edges;
- **a mountain shore**: rocky ranges and cliffs to the sea, a narrow river valley and one small bay with flat land behind it; copper and marble in many places;
- **a dry plain**: wide, open, ochre ground with low hills, olive groves and vines everywhere, a narrow green river valley, one small wood, and water under the ground only near the river and a few springs;
- **a wooded coast**: deep woods of pine and oak over the hills and down to the shore, the river and the bay in clearings.
The sea always lies to the south and the roads in come from the north, east and west edges, as before.

**What the bake gains** (`src/map/bake.ts`), each optional in the land's data, so the Bay bakes bit for bit as before:
- more than one river channel: a delta's channels branch off the river and run to the sea, a tributary joins it, a second river runs to the sea on its own. The river path holds them all, with breaks between them; the water, the bridges and the terrain already ask the path for the nearest water, so they draw and build on every channel;
- lakes: water at sea level inland, cut into the land the way the sea is (fresh water, not reached by ships);
- marsh: low wet ground with pools and thick reeds;
- dryness: less green away from the river; and where water lies under the ground (`groundwater`), for the dry plain's fountains;
- irregular islands (a wobble on an islet's ellipse);
- oak trees in a land's woods (a new plant kind at the end of the list; the loggers fell oaks as they do pines).

**Each kind's palette** (`src/render/landlook.ts`, new): a few of the ground's and water's colours mixed towards the kind's own (the dry plain's ochre, the delta's deep green and silt, the mountains' grey stone, the woods' darker greens), applied over whichever look is on (original, `-modern`, `-plain`), so all three keep working. The terrain draws marsh. The preview uses the same colours.

**Water on the dry plain.** A fountain needs water under the ground there: near the river, a lake or a spring. Its refusal says so in words, as the other building rules do; on every other land a fountain goes anywhere, as now.

**The roads in.** A land knows where its roads in come from (`roadsIn` in `src/map/landcheck.ts`, on the baked map with the game's own road rules): on each of the north, east and west edges, the point whose road reaches the city's ground soonest, and the shortest straight-run road from the nearest of them to the land's site, bridges and all. Generators place the city's site so that one edge is close (the far-roads finding: on a large land the walk in was about three months). When a city is founded from the new-game screen that road is laid at once, and the view opens over it.

**The new-game screen** (`src/ui/newgame.ts` and `newgame.css`, new), opened from the welcome card's new "Found a new city" (the card keeps "Carry on" and its other lines):
- the kinds of land, each with a line on what it is like; the three sizes; the seed as a word, with "Another" for a new one;
- a drawn preview of the land from above (hill shading, sea depths, the river and its channels), with its resources marked: copper, marble, woods, grassland, farmland, the quay sites, the roads in and the road that will be laid;
- "Found the city here", which opens the land's link; "Back" to the card.
It bakes the chosen land in the page (the same bake the game uses) and draws it on a canvas, in the panels' marble and bronze.

**Founding.** `#found.<kind>.<size>.<word>` (read in `src/game/links.ts`): a new game on that land, Greece shuffled as for any new game, the road in laid, the welcome card's steps told for that land. The word is the seed (hashed); reloading founds the same land afresh, and the link can be shared.

**Saves** already carry the land (slice 42). A save now also carries the generator's edition; each kind loads from a save (a test round-trips every kind).

**The far-zoom sea seam** at a large land's southern corners (found in slice 42's review) is looked at and closed if it is the skirt's join.

## As built

Commits: the plan `f845951`; the kinds of land `71182d9`; the new-game screen, founding links and the demos `fad7f7b`; the look, the checks and this note in the last commit.

**The kinds** (`src/map/kinds.ts`, new, about 1,000 lines). One `Sketch` of shared pieces (the shore from a baseline with bays, capes and a delta's lobe; rivers as wobbling curves; massifs, ranges, rises, headlands, islets, lakes, uplands, tributaries; woods, groves, cypresses, outcrops, herds; the fourteen districts) and a recipe per kind, each with its own salt so the same seed makes a different land of each kind:
- *sheltered bay, edition 2*: the bay anywhere on a small land and near the west or east side on a larger one (so the road in is short), four shapes (broad, deep, two coves, open), the cape on the side with more room, the massif and spring anywhere along the north, and on medium and large lands one or two of a lake, uplands (rocky hills with copper and a wood) and a tributary river.
- *islands*: a wider sea; two or three islands of 7 to 16 tiles inside the map and more beyond its edge, each with a rocky crown and pines, and small islets; a cove for the quay and a gentle second cove.
- *delta*: three or four channels fanning from the river's head to a lobe pushed 20 to 27 tiles out to sea, marsh with pools between the mouths and a marsh lagoon beside the lobe, a quay bay to one side with a rocky knoll (marble, and a smooth shoulder to the sea for the Lighthouse), copper hills at the far side; on medium and large lands a tributary and one or two lakes (with marsh on their far side) inland. On a large land the delta lies off the middle, its bay towards the nearer edge.
- *mountains*: a 34-tile lattice of rocky massifs (9 to 15 high) kept off the river, the plain behind the one small bay and the way from it to the headland; cliffs along the shore; copper and marble alternating on the ranges' flanks towards the plain, plus a low copper foothill at the plain's edge (so a mine always has somewhere gentle enough); a gentle shoulder on the headland and a low lookout on the bay's shore for the Lighthouse.
- *dry plain*: `dry: 1`; a long-bending narrow river, a rocky north ridge with copper on its lower flank, low hills each with an olive grove, one small wood by the river, two to five springs with cypresses, a headland with marble.
- *wooded coast*: `oaks: 0.45`, woods a third thicker than elsewhere (`dense: 1.3`) over most of the hills and down to the shore, the river and bay in clearings.

**The bake** (`src/map/bake.ts`): channels as extra strands of the one river path, with a `cut` flag between strands so no segment joins two of them, each strand's surface worked out on its own (a delta's branch leaves at the river's own level, a tributary is floored at the river's surface where it joins); lakes as negative distance in the land and sea mask; marsh weights, pools and reeds (reeds on a seed of their own, after every older plant); dryness scaling the general fertility; `groundwater` per tile on a dry land (by the river, the shore and the springs, `GROUNDWATER_REACH` 9 tiles); irregular islets; oaks (`TreeKind.Oak`, 8, the last kind). The hills are now filtered per row before the per-point loop (the same hills in the same order, so the same sums), which keeps a land of many hills from baking slower. The Bay's hashes and slice 42's edition 1 (three lands at 128, 192 and 256 hashed at `87f8476`) are unchanged.

**Editions.** `LAND_GEN = 2` in `generate.ts`; `generateCoast(kind, size, seed, gen)` makes edition 1 (slice 42's sheltered bay, its code untouched) or 2. A new land is stamped with its edition (`stampLand`, in `createState`); `fromSlice46` stamps `gen: 1` on a generated land from before; `landProblem` refuses an edition this game doesn't have ("made by a newer game"). `#landsmall`, `#landmedium` and `#landlarge` name `gen: 1`, so they open exactly as before. `pinLand` keeps the town's own land baked whatever the new-game screen bakes.

**Roads in** (`roadsIn` in `landcheck.ts`): Dijkstra over tile and heading from the free tile nearest the site, a turn costing 2.5 tiles and a bridge's tiles 4, bridges found with the game's own `bridgeSpan`; returns the best tile and length on each edge and the shortest road. Over the sweep below the laid road is 32 to 74 tiles on every kind at every size (the far-roads finding was about 128 on slice 42's large land).

**The new-game screen** (`src/ui/newgame.ts`, `newgame.css`, `landpreview.ts`): the seven kinds with a line each, Small / Medium / Large, the seed word with "Another" (a word list), a 448-pixel picture of the land a pixel a tile (sea depths, river and channels, farmland in rows, grass, rock, marsh, sand, marble and copper, hill shading from the north-west, timber trees and olives), marks for where the city starts, the road in, the roads in from each edge (arrows), quay sites, copper, marble, woods and springs, a legend in the land's own colours and a line of counts. "Found the city here" opens `#found.<kind>.<size>.<word>` (the look's `-modern`/`-plain` carried over); the Bay closes the screen and the card on the bare link, or opens the bare link from elsewhere. Esc and "Back" return to the card. The screen's own randomness (the word for "Another") is UI only.

**Founding** (`main.ts`, a Slice 46 block): a founding link founds a new game on its land with Greece shuffled as for any new game, lays the `roadsIn` road with the ordinary road command, and the welcome card names the land, its line and the road ("The road in is laid, 46 tiles from the east edge"). The camera opens over the site.

**Palettes** (`src/render/landlook.ts`): per kind, a few palette entries mixed towards the kind's colours over whichever look is on; applied once the town's land is known, before the world is built. The terrain draws marsh.

**The fountain rule** (`build.ts`): on a dry land a fountain needs `groundwater` under its footprint; its refusal: "Too dry for a fountain here: on the dry plain, water lies under the ground only near the river, the springs and the shore".
