## Design decisions

**The kinds of land** (`src/map/lands.ts`: `LAND_KINDS` gains `islands`, `delta`, `mountains`, `dryplain`, `woods`). Each is generated by `generateCoast(kind, size, seed)` from shared pieces (a new `src/map/kinds.ts`: the shore with its bays, capes and lobes, rivers and their channels, hills and ranges, woods, groves, outcrops, herds, districts) with its own recipe:
- **the Bay**: the hand-made coast, unchanged;
- **a sheltered bay** (edition 2): slice 42's layout made more varied: the bay anywhere along the shore and of more shapes (broad, narrow and deep, or two coves), the river rising anywhere along the north and reaching the bay from either side, the hills, woods and groves placed afresh; on larger lands a middle with features (a lake, uplands, a tributary river, an open plain);
- **islands offshore**: a wider sea with islands of every size off the coast and beyond the map's edge, a gentle shore with a cove for the quay;
- **a river delta**: low, green land; the river splits near the sea into channels that fan out to a coast pushed out into the sea, with reeds, marsh and pools between them; bridges join the parts; the hills, copper and marble at the edges;
- **a mountain shore**: rocky ranges and cliffs to the sea, a narrow river valley and one small bay with flat land behind it; copper and marble in many places;
- **a dry plain**: wide, open, ochre ground with low hills, olive groves and vines everywhere, a narrow green river valley, one small wood, and water under the ground only near the river and a few springs;
- **a wooded coast**: deep woods of pine and oak over the hills and down to the shore, the river and the bay in clearings.
The sea always lies to the south and the roads in come from the north, east and west edges, as before.

**What the bake gains** (`src/map/bake.ts`), each optional in the land's data, so the Bay bakes bit for bit as before:
- more than one river channel: a delta's channels branch off the river and run to the sea, a tributary joins it, a second river runs to the sea on its own. The river path holds them all, with breaks between them; the water, the bridges and the terrain already ask the path for the nearest water, so they draw and build on every channel;
- lakes: water at sea level inland, cut into the land the way the sea is (fresh water, not reached by ships);
- marsh: low wet ground with pools and thick reeds;
- dryness: less green away from the river; and where water lies under the ground (`groundwater`), for the dry plain's fountains;
- irregular islands (a wobble on an islet's ellipse);
- oak trees in a land's woods (a new plant kind at the end of the list; the loggers fell oaks as they do pines).

**Each kind's palette** (`src/render/landlook.ts`, new): a few of the ground's and water's colours mixed towards the kind's own (the dry plain's ochre, the delta's deep green and silt, the mountains' grey stone, the woods' darker greens), applied over whichever look is on (original, `-modern`, `-plain`), so all three keep working. The terrain draws marsh. The preview uses the same colours.

**Water on the dry plain.** A fountain needs water under the ground there: near the river, a lake or a spring. Its refusal says so in words, as the other building rules do; on every other land a fountain goes anywhere, as now.

**The roads in.** A land knows where its roads in come from (`roadsIn` in `src/map/landcheck.ts`, on the baked map with the game's own road rules): on each of the north, east and west edges, the point whose road reaches the city's ground soonest, and the shortest straight-run road from the nearest of them to the land's site, bridges and all. Generators place the city's site so that one edge is close (the far-roads finding: on a large land the walk in was about three months). When a city is founded from the new-game screen that road is laid at once, and the view opens over it.

**The new-game screen** (`src/ui/newgame.ts` and `newgame.css`, new), opened from the welcome card's new "Found a new city" (the card keeps "Carry on" and its other lines):
- the kinds of land, each with a line on what it is like; the three sizes; the seed as a word, with "Another" for a new one;
- a drawn preview of the land from above (hill shading, sea depths, the river and its channels), with its resources marked: copper, marble, woods, grassland, farmland, the quay sites, the roads in and the road that will be laid;
- "Found the city here", which opens the land's link; "Back" to the card.
It bakes the chosen land in the page (the same bake the game uses) and draws it on a canvas, in the panels' marble and bronze.

**Founding.** `#found.<kind>.<size>.<word>` (read in `src/game/links.ts`): a new game on that land, Greece shuffled as for any new game, the road in laid, the welcome card's steps told for that land. The word is the seed (hashed); reloading founds the same land afresh, and the link can be shared.

**Saves** already carry the land (slice 42). A save now also carries the generator's edition; each kind loads from a save (a test round-trips every kind).

**The far-zoom sea seam** at a large land's southern corners (found in slice 42's review) is looked at and closed if it is the skirt's join.
